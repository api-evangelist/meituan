# Meituan

> Meituan (美团) is a Chinese technology-retail company operating one of the world's largest on-demand local-services and food-delivery super-apps. Its partner integration APIs (merchant, delivery/logistics, and local-services) are exposed through a developer/partner cooperation center and are partner-gated and documented in Chinese; no public OpenAPI specification, SDK registry, or self-serve developer signup is surfaced at this time.

## Company
- [Website](https://meituan.com): Meituan corporate and consumer super-app site.
- [Developer / Partner Cooperation Center](https://developer.meituan.com): Meituan Technical Service Cooperation Center (美团技术服务合作中心), partner-gated developer portal.
